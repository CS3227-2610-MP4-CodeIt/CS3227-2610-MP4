# Approved product requirements

## Student quiz list
P-001: Show only the authenticated student's assigned published quizzes,
including quizzes from a former class when the publication roster includes them.
Each card shows title, class, question count and attempt status.
Not started and in-progress cards open the attempt; submitted cards open results.
The existing green classroom theme is shared with other roles.

## Attempts and results
P-002: A student can start or reopen their one attempt. Reopening restores saved
answers without resetting them. Other students cannot access that attempt.
P-003: Only after their own submission can students read their score, answer key
and explanations. Submitted attempts are immutable.
P-004: Account role and attempt ownership are enforced by the backend.

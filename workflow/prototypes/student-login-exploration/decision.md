# Student login prototype decision

## Selected direction

The user selected **Option 3: Course-first box** from the prototype at `index.html`.

Combine the earlier third concept's course-first brand panel and supporting copy with the boxier login form treatment from option 2. Keep the existing CodeIt blue and slate palette, current login copy, and centered login task. The brand panel's “C” mark is exploratory placeholder artwork, not an approved or official CodeIt logo; replace it with the actual CodeIt wordmark/logo if available during implementation.

## Scope

This is a design reference for the student login screen only. Keep the prototype isolated from production code. The prototype's comparison controls and alternate concepts are for exploration and do not belong in the shipped page. Maintain the existing login behavior and shared role styling outside the student login surface.
